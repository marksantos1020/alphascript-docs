The opposite view to the discount strategy: buy when the pool price moves above the protocol's moving price with
momentum.

**Entry.** The close crosses above the moving price while it is also above an EMA of the close.

**Exit.** The close crosses back below the moving price, or falls more than *Min premium %* below it.

**What to try.** Run it next to *Moving-price discount* on the same subnet: one tends to work when the other does not.
